Parameter Definitions

What each parameter measures and the evidence that shows strength.

X — Product Capability
  1. NPWT Pump Technology & Performance
    The pump's ability to deliver consistent negative pressure, manage exudate, and offer portability and battery life. Strength is shown by published technical specs, reliability data, and user ergonomics.
  2. Product Portfolio Breadth & Configurations
    Range of NPWT systems offered, including single-use, reusable, and ultra-portable devices for different care settings. Strength is evidenced by the number of distinct product lines and their clinical indications.
  3. Wound Interface & Dressing Innovation
    Advanced dressings, foams, and interfaces that optimize wound healing and reduce complications. Strength is demonstrated by patented designs, clinical outcomes, and ease of use.
  4. Digital Connectivity & Data Integration
    Features that enable remote monitoring, data logging, and integration with electronic health records. Strength is shown by connectivity options, software platforms, and interoperability certifications.
  5. Clinical Evidence & Regulatory Approvals
    Robust clinical studies and regulatory clearances (e.g., FDA, CE) supporting safety and efficacy. Strength is evidenced by peer-reviewed publications, trial results, and approved indications.
Y — Business Capability
  1. Installed Base & Global Distribution Reach
    Number of active NPWT devices in use and the geographic footprint of sales and service. Strength is shown by installed base figures, number of countries served, and distributor networks.
  2. Reimbursement Coverage & Payer Contracts
    Extent of reimbursement by public and private payers for NPWT products. Strength is evidenced by coverage policies, contract wins, and favorable payment rates.
  3. Revenue Scale & Growth in NPWT
    Annual revenue attributable to NPWT products and its growth rate. Strength is shown by financial reports, market share estimates, and year-over-year growth.
  4. Key Customer Contracts & Partnerships
    Named contracts with hospitals, home care providers, and group purchasing organizations. Strength is evidenced by contract values, duration, and strategic alliances.
  5. Manufacturing Capacity & Supply Chain
    Production capabilities and supply chain resilience for NPWT devices and consumables. Strength is shown by manufacturing facilities, capacity expansions, and supply agreements.
