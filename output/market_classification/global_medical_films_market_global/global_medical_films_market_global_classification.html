Parameter Definitions

What each parameter measures and the evidence that shows strength.

X — Product Capability
  1. Film Purity & Biocompatibility
    Measures the film's compliance with USP Class VI, ISO 10993, and low extractables/leachables for patient safety. Strength shown by certifications and third-party test reports.
  2. Barrier & Sterilization Performance
    Evaluates the film's ability to maintain sterile barrier against oxygen, moisture, and microbes, and withstand gamma, EtO, or autoclave sterilization. Strength evidenced by validated barrier data and sterilization compatibility.
  3. Material Innovation & R&D
    Assesses development of novel polymers, multilayer structures, and coatings that improve flexibility, clarity, or drug compatibility. Strength indicated by patents, white papers, and new product launches.
  4. Manufacturing Precision & Capacity
    Refers to tight thickness tolerance, defect-free production, and scalable output in cleanroom environments. Strength shown by ISO 13485 certification, capacity figures, and low defect rates.
  5. Product Portfolio Breadth
    Covers the range of film types (e.g., PE, PVC, TPU, multilayer) and formats (rolls, pouches, sheets) for diverse medical applications. Strength demonstrated by catalog depth and custom engineering capabilities.
Y — Business Capability
  1. Regulatory Approvals & Certifications
    Tracks FDA 510(k), CE Mark, and other market-specific clearances for medical films. Strength evidenced by number of approved products and countries with regulatory acceptance.
  2. Customer Contracts & OEM Partnerships
    Measures signed supply agreements with medical device manufacturers, pharmaceutical companies, and healthcare providers. Strength shown by named customers, contract duration, and exclusivity.
  3. Revenue & Market Share
    Quantifies annual sales revenue and global or regional market share in medical films. Strength indicated by consistent growth, high revenue, and leading position versus competitors.
  4. Global Manufacturing & Distribution Footprint
    Assesses number of production sites, distribution centers, and countries served. Strength shown by geographic diversity, local presence, and logistics capabilities.
  5. R&D Investment & Commercial Pipeline
    Tracks R&D spending as percentage of revenue and number of products in development or recently launched. Strength evidenced by pipeline size, commercialization rate, and revenue from new products.
