# Market relevance audit (own web search)

Date: 2026-08-13  
Markets: Flexible Packaging (157), Wearable Medical Devices (230)

Criterion used:
- **Flexible packaging:** film / pouch / laminate converters, packaging films makers (not apparel adhesives, not pure distributors, not tear-tape-only unless clearly flex-pack OEM).
- **Wearable medical devices:** OEM of body-worn / sensor medical or clinical-grade wearable monitoring (not IT services, not pure semiconductor connectors, not consumer audio/fitness-only gadgets, not non-wearable diagnostics).

---

## Verdict

| Market | Related? | Summary |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Flexible Packaging | **Mostly yes** (~90%+) | Core list is real converters/film makers. A few wrong or weak. |
| Wearable Medical | **No — many unrelated** | Large IT / consumer-gadget / component / non-wearable diagnostics noise. |

## Rough counts (hand audit + web spot-checks)

## Recommendation

1. Re-filter Wearable Medical with a strict OEM rule: “sells a wearable medical / clinical monitoring device under own brand.”
2. Drop IT services and India consumer smartwatch brands.
3. Flexible Packaging: drop Bemis Associates, Macfarlane, Essentra plc; de-dupe Constantia/Taghleef/Essel/Schur/Mitsubishi.
