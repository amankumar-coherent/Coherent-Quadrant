Parameter Definitions

What each scoring parameter means for Global Liquefied Natural Gas Market (generated for this market — not a fixed template).

X — LNG Asset & Operations Strength
Liquefaction Capacity & Utilization
Total nameplate liquefaction capacity (mtpa) and actual utilization rates. High capacity and utilization indicate strong operational scale and efficiency.
Feedstock Supply Security
Access to reliable natural gas feedstocks, including long-term supply agreements and upstream integration. Secure feedstock ensures stable operations and cost control.
Asset Reliability & Uptime
Track record of plant availability, on-stream time, and maintenance performance. High uptime minimizes production losses and maximizes revenue.
Safety & Regulatory Compliance
Safety performance (e.g., TRIR) and adherence to environmental and operational regulations. Strong compliance reduces operational risk and enhances reputation.
Carbon Intensity & Efficiency
Greenhouse gas emissions per tonne of LNG produced and energy efficiency of liquefaction trains. Lower carbon intensity improves market access and meets decarbonization goals.
Y — Commercial & Portfolio Scale
LNG Sales & Contract Portfolio
Volume of LNG contracted under long-term and spot agreements, including contract duration and pricing terms. A robust portfolio ensures revenue stability and market presence.
Customer & Geographic Diversification
Spread of customers across regions and markets. Diversification reduces dependence on any single buyer or region, mitigating demand risk.
Financial Performance & Liquidity
Revenue, EBITDA, cash flow, and debt metrics. Strong financials enable investment in growth and resilience to market volatility.
Growth Project Pipeline
Planned or under-construction liquefaction and regasification projects, including capacity additions and timelines. A robust pipeline indicates future growth potential.
Strategic Partnerships & Market Position
Joint ventures, equity stakes, and long-term supply deals with major players. Strong partnerships enhance market access and competitive positioning.
