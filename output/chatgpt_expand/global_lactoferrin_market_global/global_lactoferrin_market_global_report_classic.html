Market Type
B2B — The Global Lactoferrin Market covers lactoferrin ingredients and products derived from bovine, human, recombinant and other sources, used across nutritional, pharmaceutical, food and beverage, animal nutrition and personal care applications. Lactoferrin is an iron-binding glycoprotein valued for its immune-supporting, antimicrobial and nutritional properties. The market includes lactoferrin supplied in powder, liquid, capsule, tablet and other forms, in standard (70-90%), high-purity (90-98%) and ultra-high-purity (98% and above) grades, for infant formula, dietary supplements, functional foods and beverages, medical nutrition, pharmaceutical formulations, animal nutrition and cosmetics and personal care, sold direct and through distributors.
B2B: the buyers are infant formula, supplement, food, pharmaceutical, feed and cosmetic makers that add lactoferrin to their products.

2
Secondary and Primary Research

3
Screening

Companies whose main business is an adjacent segment are listed but not placed on the chart.
→
4
Evidence Compilation
Every screened company was assessed on all ten parameters; for chart companies, evidence was recorded parameter by parameter. Scoring
Each parameter is rated 0–100 against its written definition, based on the evidence available. Ratings are analyst assessments under this methodology, not independently audited figures. X and Y are the simple average of the five parameter ratings on each axis; overall is the average of X and Y.

Normalization & Selection
